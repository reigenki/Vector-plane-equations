About this project

These simulations are early prototypes for an A-Level study app aimed at Junior College students in Singapore who struggle to start and stick with consistent revision. The focus of the first prototype is H2 Mathematics, turning key topics into interactive, low-friction activities.

  • All labs run entirely in the browser (HTML, CSS, JavaScript)
  • No build step or internet connection required after the first load
  • Designed for laptops (Windows, macOS, Chromebooks)
  • Aligned with SEAB H2 Mathematics Syllabus 9758
